Why turn a blog post into a video

Your post already has the hard part: an argument, examples and a point. A short narrated video lets that same work reach people who scroll past text, on YouTube, LinkedIn, Reels, TikTok and Shorts, without you filming or editing anything.

How it works

  1. Point it at the post. In Claude Code, run /storyboard https://yourblog.com/your-post --duration 60s.
  2. Claude plans and writes. It reads the post, picks a narrative shape (problem, frame, solution; or hook, examples, synthesis), splits it into beats and writes narration timed to your target length.
  3. Voice and animation. The free voice reads the script with exact slide timing, and Claude choreographs each beat: kinetic headlines, charts for your numbers, transitions at the real turning points.
  4. Render. You get an MP4, plus every source file in case you want to tweak a line and re-render.

Tips for posts that become great videos

  • Pick the length for the channel. 30 to 60 seconds for social, about 2 minutes for YouTube or your own site.
  • Go vertical for Reels, TikTok and Shorts with --aspect 9:16.
  • Numbers make great beats. Stats from your post become counters and charts that draw themselves on the word that names them.
  • It won't invent facts. The skill sticks to what's in your source; if the post is thin, it asks for more instead of padding.

What you get

A project folder with concept.md (the plan), script.md (the narration), storyboard.html (the animated deck you can preview in a browser), voiceover.mp3 and the rendered MP4.
