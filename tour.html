How this video was made

The tour is a normal Easy Storyboard project. Claude wrote concept.md and script.md, the free voice tool generated the narration with exact slide cues and per-word timestamps, and every animation was choreographed in storyboard.html to land on the word that names it. The engine then rendered it frame by frame to MP4, with sound effects placed automatically on transitions and celebrations.

The look is the flat "Generative Art Platform" theme: a light canvas, near-black ink and one pink accent.
